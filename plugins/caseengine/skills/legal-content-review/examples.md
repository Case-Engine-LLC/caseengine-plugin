# Content Examples - Wrong vs Correct

Reference examples showing common violations and correct alternatives.

---

## Quick Comparison Examples

### Banned Phrases

| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "For example, medical bills can accumulate quickly after an accident." | "Medical bills accumulate rapidly after crashes, including ambulance transport, diagnostic imaging, surgical procedures, and rehabilitation costs." |
| "Our expert attorneys fight for maximum compensation." | "Our experienced attorneys work to recover full compensation for your injuries." |
| "Injuries—including broken bones—require extensive treatment." | "Injuries, including broken bones, require extensive treatment and rehabilitation." |
| "When you're injured in an accident, you need legal help." | "You need legal help when injuries from an accident create mounting expenses." |
| "Navigating the complex legal landscape requires expertise." | "Filing an injury claim requires understanding specific court procedures and deadlines." |
| "In conclusion, contact us today for help." | "Contact us today to schedule your free consultation." |
| "For instance, a broken leg may require surgery." | "Broken legs often require surgical intervention, physical therapy, and months of rehabilitation." |
| "Our specialist attorneys have expertise in personal injury law." | "Our attorneys bring decades of trial experience to personal injury cases." |

## Full Section Example - Compensation Type

### Wrong Version

```markdown
### Medical Expenses

Medical bills are important. For example, hospital visits can cost a lot. Our expert attorneys will fight for maximum compensation for your medical expenses. When you're injured, navigating the complex legal landscape is difficult. In conclusion, contact us today.
```

### Correct Version

```markdown
### **1. Medical Expenses**

**Severe**

**Settlement Range**

$1,000-$1,500,000+

Emergency room visits, surgeries, and ongoing treatments create substantial damages following pedestrian accident collisions in Santa Ana. Hospital bills accumulate rapidly after crashes, including ambulance transport, diagnostic imaging, surgical procedures, and rehabilitation costs. California law allows recovery of all past, present, and future **medical expenses after pedestrian accidents.** Attorneys document treatment through medical records and negotiate with providers to maximize your net recovery.

*Contact us today to schedule your free consultation to discuss your medical treatment expenses.*
```

**Why the correct version works:**
- No "for example" - examples integrated naturally
- No em-dashes - uses commas instead
- No "maximum compensation" - focuses on actual damages
- First sentence incorporates the heading keyword "Medical Expenses"
- Specific details: "ambulance transport, diagnostic imaging, surgical procedures"
- Present tense: "create", "accumulate", "allows"
- Bold only used for anchor text phrase, not random words
- CTA is italicized

## Full Introduction Example

### Correct Introduction Style

```markdown
# **Santa Ana Pedestrian Accident Attorney**

Victims of pedestrian collisions in Santa Ana face mounting medical bills, lost wages, and insurance companies that routinely deny valid claims or offer settlements far below actual costs. California Vehicle Code provisions place specific duties on drivers to yield to pedestrians in crosswalks and at intersections, yet proving liability requires thorough accident scene documentation, witness statements, traffic camera footage analysis, and medical records linking injuries directly to the collision. Orange County pedestrian cases become complicated when multiple parties share fault, when drivers lack adequate insurance coverage, or when victims delay seeking legal counsel while evidence disappears and statutory deadlines approach.

Pedestrian accident lawyers conduct independent investigations to establish driver negligence, review police reports for inaccuracies, consult medical professionals who document injury severity and long-term prognosis, and file claims against all responsible parties including drivers, vehicle owners, and potentially negligent businesses. Legal representation protects victims from insurance adjusters who pressure early settlements before full injury extent becomes clear, handles all communication with opposing parties to prevent recorded statements that undermine claims, and builds compelling cases through accident reconstruction experts and economic damage calculations. Santa Ana attorneys familiar with Orange County Superior Court procedures pursue fair recovery for emergency treatment costs, ongoing rehabilitation expenses, permanent disability accommodations, and non-economic damages while meeting California's two-year statute of limitations for personal injury actions.

The benefits of hiring a Santa Ana pedestrian accident lawyer are listed below:

* Thorough investigation of crosswalk violations, traffic signal timing, driver distraction evidence, and roadway hazard conditions with evidence preservation
* Professional analysis of emergency room records, orthopedic evaluations, neurological assessments, and long-term disability projections
* Fair compensation pursuit for medical expenses, lost income, future care needs, and pain and suffering damages
```

**Notice:**
- Leads with sharp fact (mounting bills, denied claims)
- No "for example" or weather/time opening
- Long sentences with multiple clauses
- Specific references (CVC, Orange County Superior Court)
- Present tense throughout
- Bullet list with parallel structure (all start with noun phrases)

## Injury Type Section Example

### Correct Format

```markdown
### Traumatic Brain Injuries

**Severe – Neurological**

**Settlement Range**

$50,000 - $5,000,000+

**Duration:** 6-24 months; permanent effects possible

Traumatic brain injuries (TBI) occur when violent impacts cause the brain to collide with the skull during pedestrian accidents.

**Common Symptoms**
- Persistent headaches and migraines
- Memory loss and confusion
- Difficulty concentrating
- Mood changes and irritability
- Sleep disturbances
- Sensitivity to light and sound

**Claim Impact**

TBI claims require extensive neurological documentation and often involve ongoing treatment costs that insurance companies dispute.

**Defense Strategy**

Insurers argue pre-existing conditions caused symptoms or that injuries are exaggerated without objective medical evidence.

**Settlement Value Factors**
- Severity documented by Glasgow Coma Scale score
- Duration of loss of consciousness
- Documented cognitive impairment through neuropsychological testing

**Required Medical Reports**
- CT and MRI imaging results
- Neurologist evaluation
- Neuropsychological assessment

**Legal Considerations**

**Brain injury claims** require specialized medical testimony, particularly when symptoms persist beyond initial treatment periods.

**Win Rate:** 78%
```

## Common Mistakes Summary

| Category | Mistake | Fix |
|----------|---------|-----|
| Example phrases | "For example, X..." | "X, including A, B, and C." |
| Attorney descriptors | "expert attorneys" | "experienced attorneys" |
| Compensation language | "maximum recovery" | "full recovery" |
| Punctuation | "injuries — severe ones — require" | "injuries, including severe ones, require" |
| Clause placement | "When injured, you need..." | "You need X when injured." |
| Vague stats | "many accidents occur" | "1,237 crashes annually according to TxDOT" |
| Generic content | "our qualified team" | "Attorney John Smith, with 15 years of trial experience" |
| AI tell-phrases | "navigate the legal landscape" | "file claims in Orange County Superior Court" |
