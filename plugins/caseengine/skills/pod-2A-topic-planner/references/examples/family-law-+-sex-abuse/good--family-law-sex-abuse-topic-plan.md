## Methodology: How topics are selected

Topic selection runs on four inputs. First, a map of the firm's practice areas covering every sub-topic and case-law thread that signals real expertise. Second, search demand data showing what Maryland audiences actually type into Google, what triggers AI Overviews, and where the firm has the cleanest path to rank. Third, the firm's own priorities: which practice areas drive revenue today, which are the differentiators the firm wants to be known for, and which audiences are worth the investment. Fourth, a virality pass for topics that pull weight on YouTube and social, not just on Google.

The result is twelve episodes sequenced to compound trust, search visibility, and qualified case flow. Coverage is intentional, not exhaustive. The right topics in the right order beat a long list of mediocre ones every time.
